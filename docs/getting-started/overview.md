## First Steps

### Day One

1. **Set up your accounts**
   - Email and calendar
   - Communication tools
   - Development environments

2. **Meet your team**
   - Schedule introductions with team members
   - Join relevant team channels
   - Review team structure

3. **Access documentation**
   - Bookmark this documentation site
   - Review relevant sections for your role
   - Familiarize yourself with company policies

1. **Complete onboarding tasks**
   - Review security policies
   - Set up development environment
   - Complete required training

2. **Learn the tools**
   - Communication platforms
   - Project management tools
   - Development tools and systems
